# Preset provenance

One entry per factory preset (PRESETS_PROMPT.md §7.4, VISION.md "Presets"; linked from `docs/ASSETS.md`). Every preset is **designed from scratch** with Cartwave's own macros (volume, arpeggio and its speed, pitch and duty sequences, vibrato, the channel controls, and on studio presets Unison / Detune / Drift). **Nothing is copied from games, ROMs, ripped patches or any third-party library**, and no name refers to a game, console maker or brand. Author of every preset: **Jeffrey Scott Frederick**. Licence: MIT OR Apache-2.0, as the code.

- **New bank** (2026-11-27): generated by [`presets/tools/design_bank.py`](tools/design_bank.py) into [`presets/bank.json`](bank.json), level-matched with `crates/ui/examples/bank_pack.rs` (gains in `presets/levels.json`).
- **Classic presets** (2026-11-25, Batch 1): the 60 hand-written presets in `crates/chip/src/presets.rs`, folded into the bank in each role's Classic folder (RUNLOG PB-D5), unchanged.

Written by `cargo run --release -p ui --example bank_pack -- --write`.
