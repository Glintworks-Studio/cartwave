# Polish 2 §6.2: every message, toast and notice

> *Historical report, kept as written under the working title ChipDAW. Since the rename (2026-11-27, [RENAME.md](../../RENAME.md)) the app is Cartwave by Glintworks: projects save as `.cartwave`, the executable is `cartwave`, and environment variables are `CARTWAVE_*` (the `CHIPDAW_*` names still work).*

Generated by `docs/polish2/tools/notices.py` from `crates/ui/src`. **Before:** all of these showed in the floating notice capsule (bottom-right), with the audio problem text and the recovery offer. **Now:**

- every `set_status` message below is a **status-bar message** (left side, 8 s, then the idle text "Saved · name"), for example "Saved", the export report, autosave recovery, colour applied, an imported file;
- audio problems (no device, can't open, reconnecting) and dropouts are **persistent problems**: a warning icon on the status bar until they're resolved; a click lists them;
- "Audio: now playing on …" after a device change is a status-bar message;
- the **recovery offer** (RECOVER / DISCARD) stays in the capsule: it's a decision;
- native **dialogs** stay for decisions only (save prompts, the MIDI-drop conflict, profile changes, anything destructive): listed at the end.
